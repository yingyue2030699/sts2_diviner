# Doomed and Revelation Balance Update

## English

- Escape from Destiny now costs 1, gains 1 Countdown of Destiny, and Exhausts. Escape from Destiny+ costs 0.
- Removed Escape from Destiny's former per-card combat cost increase.
- The first Doomed trigger each combat now shuffles 3 Escape from Destiny cards into the draw pile and 3 into the discard pile.
- The Final Strand now prevents Revelation card effects from reducing Destiny for the rest of combat.
- Updated English and Simplified Chinese descriptions without duplicating automatic Exhaust or Innate keywords.

## 简体中文

- “逃离命劫”现在费用为 1，获得 1 层命运倒计时并消耗；“逃离命劫+”费用为 0。
- 移除了“逃离命劫”原有的单卡战斗内费用递增。
- 每场战斗中第一次触发劫兆时，将 3 张“逃离命劫”洗入抽牌堆，并将 3 张洗入弃牌堆。
- “终末命缕”现在会使启示卡牌效果在本场战斗中不再降低命运。
- 已同步更新英文和简体中文描述，并避免重复显示系统自动添加的“消耗”或“固有”关键词。
