# Diviner Workshop Update - 2026-07-31

## Cards

- Evil Eye now gains 2 Energy during Bad Omen.
- Resonation of Fate costs 1 in both versions.
- Second Sight now Retains, Scries 5/6, then draws until the player has 5/6 cards.
- Omen of Fallen Sky now deals 18X/22X Foretell damage.
- The Last Word and Line of Fate now use the base-game Fatal rule and exclude minions.
- Unasked Question now loses 4/2 HP, and Foretold Falter costs 1/0.

## Potions

- Tar of Dread is Rare and fills the hand with Misfortune.
- Brew of Brew grants its extra healing and Destiny only for other potions it discards.
- Blood of the Martyr heals 60% of Max HP and sets Destiny to 0.
- Condensed Misfortune adds Misfortune+.

## Relics

- Destined Crystal Ball now correctly replaces Crystal Ball when upgraded by Touch of Orobas.
- Piedra del Sol grants +3; Knocked Compass applies 1 Weak and 1 Vulnerable during Bad Omen.
- Hourglass of Mercy is Common; Brass Dowsing Rod and Fated Contract are Uncommon.
- Fated Contract sets Destiny to 4 after combat when it is lower, while disabling reward-rarity improvement at Destiny 4 or higher.
- Last Prophecy now consumes 9 random Divination records and restores the player to 10% HP once per combat.
- Recognized keywords and status effects in relic descriptions now provide interactive tooltips.

English and Simplified Chinese localization, design documents, and regression validation were updated together.
